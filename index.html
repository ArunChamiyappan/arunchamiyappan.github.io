About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras.

My research focuses on accelerated modeling of rapid directional solidification using phase-field methods and multiscale modeling of additive manufacturing using the ICME approach.

I am passionate about developing high-performance computing solutions using GPU acceleration to solve complex problems in materials science. My work aims to bridge the gap between computational modeling and real-world manufacturing applications.
