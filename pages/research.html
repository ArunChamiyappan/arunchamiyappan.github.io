Research Interests

Accelerated Modeling of Rapid Directional Solidification

Using Phase-Field Methods to simulate microstructure evolution during rapid solidification processes. This work involves developing high-performance solvers to capture the complex interface dynamics and solute trapping effects that occur at high solidification velocities.

Multiscale Modeling of Additive Manufacturing

Applying the ICME (Integrated Computational Materials Engineering) approach to link process parameters to microstructure and properties in additive manufacturing. This involves bridging different length scales, from the melt pool dynamics to the grain structure evolution.
